Deep-Sea Volcanism and Glacial Cycles

Langmuir Lab · Harvard EPS · Ongoing
GeochemistryLA-ICP-MSSpectral analysis

Processing sediment cores and analyzing basalt-glass compositions to study whether glacial sea-level cycles influence ocean-ridge volcanism. As ice sheets grow and shrink, falling sea levels may change the pressure on mid-ocean ridges and the rate at which they erupt; the geochemistry preserved in volcanic glass helps test that link.
